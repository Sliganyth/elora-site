Elora privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Elora is a personal assistant used only by its developer, Alexander Lichtert, on his own computer. No one else signs in to it.

What it uses

  • Gmail: reads messages to find parcel deliveries, and creates drafts. It never sends mail.
  • Google Drive: reads files, and adds new files. It never changes or deletes existing files.
  • YouTube: reads which channels are followed and their newest videos.

Where the data goes

Google data is processed on that one computer, and sign-in tokens are stored there, encrypted. Short passages may be sent to Anthropic's Claude to summarize or answer something the user asked. Nothing is sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
